## Homepage

Most candidates are portrait photos. Try IMG_8246 for a finished-work image or IMG_8680 for an infrastructure-focused image in a split layout. A full-width banner needs a crop preview before selection; a new landscape photo would give more room for a headline.

## Selection notes

- Use a small balanced selection rather than all 63 images.
- Avoid near duplicates (IMG_7380/7381, IMG_8084/8085, IMG_8678/8679).
- IMG_9613 has a dark foreground obstruction; IMG_7976 includes a prominent photographer shadow. Prefer clearer alternatives.
- Confirm project names, locations and the company's role before writing project captions; the photos alone do not establish those facts.
- Website originals have not been replaced or added to pages.
